**S.P.R.U.C.E+** (Spam & Phishing Risk URL Checker Extension+) is a browser extension that helps you
spot phishing and scam emails in Gmail and other webmail. This is its first public release.

> **A helper, not a guarantee.** S.P.R.U.C.E+ makes a best effort to point out warning signs, but it
> can miss dangerous emails and links, and it can flag genuine ones. It is one small layer of
> protection: keep your webmail's own spam filtering, your organization's email security, your
> browser's protections and your own judgment.

## What it does

- Shows where links really go: short links and security-gateway wrappers are expanded, and links whose
  text shows one site but lead to another are pointed out.
- Points out warning signs in your inbox before you open anything: fake brand names, look-alike
  addresses, known scam senders, senders using your own name, "call this number" scams.
- Warns you in the opened email, with dangerous links marked in red.
- Checks QR codes and attachments on your device.
- Moves likely scams to Spam (Gmail), with your webmail's own Report spam.
- Removes tracking pixels.
- No account, no ads, no analytics. Every check has its own switch, and organizations can set or lock
  each one by policy (Chrome, Edge and Firefox).

Full details: [docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/thecodewarrior2/SPRUCE-Browser-Extension/blob/main/docs/FEATURES.md).

## For administrators

Every setting can be set and locked by policy, plus administrator-only options (hiding the Settings
button, recent activity or the Donate button; phishing-simulation emails):
[admin guide](https://github.com/thecodewarrior2/SPRUCE-Browser-Extension/blob/main/docs/ADMIN-GUIDE.md).
In Chrome and Edge, store installs have a different extension ID from manual installs; use the ID of
the version you deploy in your policies. In Firefox the ID is the same for both.
